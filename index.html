Moments

Event photo sharing, live the instant it happens

Moments turns one QR code into a shared event gallery. Guests upload photos and videos from their browser—no app, account, or registration required.

Everything your event gallery needs

  • Live slideshow: show new guest photos and videos on any screen as they arrive.
  • Public or private: use an open gallery or protect the event with a PIN and invitation link.
  • Reliable large uploads: interrupted uploads are queued, retried, and resumed when the connection returns.
  • Highlights and reactions: let guests favorite the best moments.
  • Album export: download the complete gallery or a selected set as a ZIP file.
  • Host controls: moderate content, review analytics, and manage event retention.

How Moments works

  1. Create an event and share its QR code.
  2. Guests scan, choose a name, and upload from their phones.
  3. Enjoy the live gallery, then export the memories after the event.
