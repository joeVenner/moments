Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Moments lets guests at an event share photos and videos in one album, through the website, the App Clip and the iOS app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it and how long we keep it.

Moments is run by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS] ("we"). For any privacy question or request, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

Guests don't create an account. When you join an event we keep:

  • the name you choose and your avatar (a generated drawing, not a photo of you);
  • a random guest key stored on your device (in your browser, or in the iPhone keychain), so the event remembers you;
  • the photos and videos you share, their captions, and your hearts and reports;
  • for private events, a signed pass proving you entered the PIN or invite link (valid 7 days).

Hosts also give us their event details (name, date, type, cover picture) and the sign-in needed to manage them. If a host asks for an AI cover, the photo they pick (often a portrait) is sent to our image provider to create it (see below); we keep only the cover it returns, not that photo. Subscriptions are bought through Apple; we receive the purchase status from Apple, never your card details.

Everyone: to stop abuse (PIN guessing, too many joins), we use your IP address briefly. Short-lived rate limits may check the address itself without us storing it; what we store is only a keyed hash, and we delete failed-attempt records after one day at most. Our servers' logs don't contain file names, captions or guest names.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Why we use it

  • To run the event album you joined or host: showing moments, hearts, points and the leaderboard (performing our service to you).
  • To keep events safe: private-event access, moderation, reports and abuse prevention (our legitimate interest in a safe service).
  • To manage subscriptions and their allowances (performing the contract with hosts).

Who can see it

What you share in an event is visible to the people in that event: its host and its guests. A public event can be opened by anyone with its link or QR code; a private one needs its PIN or invite link. Hosts can hide or delete any moment.

Who processes it for us

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database, photo and video storage, video streaming, and cookieless visit statistics.
  • Google Fonts: the website loads its fonts from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • OpenAI: only when a host asks for an AI cover; the chosen photo and the event theme are sent to create the image.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, the App Clip and app delivery.

These providers may process data outside your country, under their own data-protection commitments.

How long we keep it

  • An event and everything in it (moments, guests, hearts) is deleted when its hosting ends: shortly after the host deletes it, or once the time after a subscription ends has passed (30 days to download, 14 after a refund, then deletion).
  • A guest can leave an event at any time from the website, App Clip or app; this deletes the moments they shared there.
  • Hashed IP records of failed attempts: one day at most.
  • Purchase records: as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Guests can delete what they shared themselves by leaving the event. You can also complain to your data-protection authority ([SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY]).

Children

Moments isn't intended for children under [MINIMUM AGE]. Event hosts are responsible for who they invite.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and show the new date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
